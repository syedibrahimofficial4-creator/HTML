What is Sport?

Sport is any physical activity played with rules, where people compete as individuals or teams. Sports keep the body fit, build discipline and teamwork, and bring communities together. From neighbourhood grounds to giant stadiums, billions of people play and watch sport every year.

Why Sports Matter

💪

Health

Regular play strengthens the heart, muscles and mind, and lowers stress.

🤝

Teamwork

Team games teach cooperation, trust and how to win and lose with respect.

🎯

Discipline

Training and goals build focus, patience and a habit of hard work.

🌍

Unity

Sport connects people across countries, languages and cultures.
